Questions

Good to know

How long does it take?

Every painting is different, so it is impossible to give an exact timeline. The two main factors are how many commissions are ahead of yours and the size of the painting. A 30 × 40 in painting takes approximately 100 hours once the concept is agreed and paint starts going on the canvas. When it is complete, it takes 2–4 weeks to dry. That puts a 30 × 40 at roughly 4–6 weeks if no commissions are ahead of yours, not including shipping or delivery.

When the written contract is made, you will have an agreed completion date in writing, along with your options if the artist cannot meet the deadline.

In a time crunch, or need a gift sooner? For an extra charge you can get an original black-and-white graphite drawing of the planned painting to present with the promise of the painting to come.

Who decides what is in the painting?

It’s a collaboration between you and the artist. The best works of art come from inspiration deep within the artist as they create; without it, the final piece would be missing that special something. That is why Scott reserves the right to turn down commissions that don’t inspire him.

“I won’t start a painting that I am not willing to hang in my living room when it is complete.”

Scott often works from several photographs to make a single painting. You can provide photos you took yourself or that aren’t under copyright. For example, if you’d like your wedding flowers painted from your professional photographer’s pictures, you’ll need the photographer’s written permission, even though you paid for the images.

Photos aren’t required, though. What flowers were at the wedding? What colors? Scott can compose a painting from that, and you’ll see the composition before any deposit. You can also choose from flower photographs Scott has taken himself, or just name your favorite color and let him find the flower to match.

Why oil paint?

Oil paint has been the preferred medium of master artists for centuries, prized for its rich pigments, depth and longevity. It allows smooth blending and intricate detail, and its slow drying time lets the artist build layers and luminosity unmatched by other mediums. Many masterpieces from the Renaissance and beyond have survived for hundreds of years, their colors still vibrant. Properly cared for, an oil painting is a legacy that can be passed down for generations.

Scott’s paintings are typically touch-dry within 2–4 weeks, when a temporary protective retouch varnish can be applied before delivery. Oil paint can take 6 months to a year to dry fully; after that, a final varnish is recommended.

Can I get a discount?

Unlike mass-reproduced art from big-box stores, original art is an investment: a one-of-a-kind work the artist puts their heart and soul into. There is no experience like standing in front of an original and seeing the texture of each brushstroke. A printer or a screen cannot recreate that, and as AI fills the world with mass-produced images, hand-made human art becomes even more valuable.

When you commission an original oil painting from me, I will put everything I have into it, and be just as passionate about it as you are. You’re not just buying a pretty picture; you’re buying the centerpiece of your space, an investment that won’t be discounted in the future.
